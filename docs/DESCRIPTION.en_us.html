Obvia Sans is a geometric sans-serif typeface crafted to pair seamlessly with its monospaced sibling, Obvia Mono. With its clean lines and modern character, it is particularly well-suited for headlines, branding, posters, and other large-scale display applications.

Designed by Selçuk Çukur in collaboration with Obvia, the typeface reflects a philosophy of clarity, minimalism, and efficiency. Drawing inspiration from the Swiss design tradition, Obvia Sans emphasizes precision and functionality, offering designers and developers a tool that enhances communication and elevates visual impact.
